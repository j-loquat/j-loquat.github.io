Watts Daily Compass

From Fighting Life to Flowing With It

Many days feel like constant pushing, forcing, and resisting. Alan Watts suggested another way: cooperate with life. This compass turns that into a daily practice of four simple questions.

What It Is

A Simple Daily Life Strategy

Four questions, one compass for everyday decisions. Rooted in surrender, curiosity, and interconnection. Fast enough for a busy moment, deep enough to reshape your lifestyle.

The Four Questions

One Compass for Everyday Decisions

Control

Where am I forcing the uncontrollable?

This question reveals tight gripping and helps you release unwinnable battles.

Direction One

Control: Stop Fighting What You Do Not Control

  • We overestimate how much life obeys our will.
  • Letting go frees energy for what truly matters.
  • Acceptance is clear-eyed realism, not passivity.

Practice Control

  1. Notice tension, clenching, or looping anxious thoughts.
  2. Ask: What exactly here is outside my control?
  3. Release the imaginary levers you keep pulling.
  4. Redirect attention to choices available now.

Direction Two

Current: Move With, Not Against, Life

  • Life unfolds like a river, not a machine.
  • Ask: Where is energy already flowing?
  • Cooperation uses momentum instead of brute force.

Practice Current

  1. Look for doors opening and timing that feels natural.
  2. Ask: What happens if I stop pushing here?
  3. Ride opportunities instead of muscling through.
  4. Trust guidance from reality more than inner drama.

Direction Three

Teaching: Let Life Be Your Teacher

  • Every annoyance, delay, or surprise can carry a lesson.
  • Ask: What is this training?
  • Curiosity replaces resentment, and wisdom compounds.

Practice Teaching

  1. Pause when frustrated, embarrassed, or disappointed.
  2. Ask: If this were training, what for?
  3. Listen for themes like patience, boundaries, humility, courage.
  4. Capture insights quickly before they fade.

Direction Four

Belonging: Remember You Are Part of Everything

  • Watts emphasized deep interconnection.
  • Belonging shifts focus from self-obsession to participation.
  • Contribution replaces isolation as your default orientation.

Practice Belonging

  1. Ask: How can I add value right here?
  2. Offer presence, listening, or encouragement.
  3. Notice how small acts ripple through your circles.
  4. Belonging grows when you behave as though you belong.

How They Work Together

A Continuous Loop

  • Control frees energy by dropping unwinnable battles.
  • Current redirects energy toward aligned opportunities.
  • Teaching converts daily experience into wisdom.
  • Belonging channels wisdom into caring contribution.

A Day With the Compass

Morning, Daytime, Evening

  1. Morning: Glance at all four questions.
  2. Daytime: Recall whichever question fits the moment.
  3. Evening: Review how you moved through them.

Always in Light of Your Values

Direction, Not Perfection

The compass is not conformity. Your values define what "moving with the current" means for your life. Ask: Did I honor what matters most today?

Small loyal steps compound into a coherent life.

When You Forget

Return Gently

Forgetting is normal. Noticing you forgot is progress. Difficult days are rich teaching material. Rewrite the day with the four questions and continue with a kind, playful tone.
